- **FINDING (deferred, perf): note-name autocomplete churn.** `completions.ts:32` `createWikilinkCompletion` matches `/\[\[[^\]]*$/` — fires even *after* the `|` (during type selection) and runs `n.name.toLowerCase()` over all ~7,600 notes every keystroke (≈7,600 throwaway allocations/keystroke, zero matches since names have no `|`).
