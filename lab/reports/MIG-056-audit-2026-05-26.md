## §4 — §J.1 Hotfix Bundle

Two P1 findings warranted inline fix before §K Boss-test:

### §J.1.A — Federation generation counter (race fix)

**Files:**
- `src-tauri/src/search.rs` — `SearchState` gains `federation_generation: AtomicU64`; `invalidate_search_state` increments it (SeqCst ordering); background-thread spawn captures pre-spawn generation, checks before writing.
- `src-tauri/src/search.rs` — two test-helper `SearchState` constructions updated to include the new field.

**Why:** Without the counter, fast user-driven universe switches during the ~10-100ms background-attach window leave SearchState with a mismatched (OLD connection, NEW FederationContext) pair. The counter detects the switch and abandons stale work.

### §J.1.B — Lens fallback parity

**Files:**
- `src-tauri/src/lens/query.rs::execute_lens` — federated-then-fallback inline closure pattern. On any error from `execute_federated_query` (federated_conn None due to race; transient lock; etc.), log + fall through to single-schema. Matches sibling consumers (`aggregate_library_counts`, `federated_lexical_search_or_fallback`).

**Why:** The §J drift audit's P1-1 finding. Lens was the odd one out — failed instead of degrading.

### Verification post-§J.1

- `cargo test --lib federation::` → 42/42 still passing.
- `cargo test --lib lens::` → 84/84 still passing.
- No regression in libraries / search tests.
