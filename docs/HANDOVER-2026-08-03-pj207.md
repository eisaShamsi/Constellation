## 5 · The thing that changed how work reaches the Boss

After I described two screens I had not opened, the Boss mandated a pipeline. **It is law,
in `CLAUDE.md`, and it is not skippable:**

```
Agent({ subagent_type: 'tutorial-auditor', prompt: '<what changed + what is observable>' })
Agent({ subagent_type: 'ui-inspector',     prompt: '<the complete draft, verbatim>' })
```

Nothing reaches him unapproved, and any edit I make re-enters at the inspector.

**Across §6 and §7 they caught 16 findings in test material I would have sent after one
read-through** — including a step whose opening screen was inverted for his actual

## 6 · What to internalise before touching this code

**"A builder is not a healer."** The plan was wrong three times in the same shape: a
back-fill computes and *then* stamps, so routing it through the healer's stamp gate makes
it a permanent no-op. That plan had been through an Architect phase and two adversarial
reviews. **Verify it against the code at every step anyway.**

**And the §7 review's lesson about my own work:** both HIGH findings were safety nets I
disarmed *while building them* — a marker that cleared itself on the failure it existed
for, and a single-flight flag with no RAII against this codebase's own precedent. **A guard
you have just written is exactly the guard you are least likely to check.**
