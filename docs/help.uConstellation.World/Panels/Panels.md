# Panels

Constellation's information panels — Backlinks, Outgoing Links, Properties, Tags, Sky View, Tasks, Calendar, Knowledge Health, Provenance, and Review Pulse — can each be placed in one of four positions via **Settings → Panels**. (The former Link Dashboard panel retired into the Constellation Circulatory System, a full-page view opened from the left dock.)

---

## Placing panels

Open **Settings → Panels** to configure where each panel lives.

Each panel has a placement selector with four options:

| Slot | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Left of note** | A column to the left of your editor. Ideal for Backlinks — the sources that point *into* the current note. |
| **Right of note** | A column to the right of your editor. Ideal for Outgoing Links — the targets your note points *out to*. |
| **Right sidebar** | The tab strip on the right side of the window (default for most panels). |
| **Hidden** | The panel is removed from the interface entirely. |

### Default layout

By default, Constellation places the two link panels as flanking columns beside the note — mimicking how the note sits at the center of its own link network:

| Panel | Default placement |
|-------|-------------------|
| Backlinks | Left of note |
| Outgoing Links | Right of note |
| Properties | Right sidebar |
| Tags | Right sidebar |
| Sky View | Right sidebar |
| Tasks | Right sidebar |
| Calendar | Right sidebar |
| Knowledge Health | Right sidebar |
| Provenance | Right sidebar |
| Review Pulse | Right sidebar |

To reset everything to defaults, click **Reset to default** at the bottom of the Panels settings section.

---

## Flanking columns

When **Backlinks** is set to *Left of note* or **Outgoing Links** is set to *Right of note*, a flanking column appears beside the editor.

### Resizing flanking columns

Drag the thin resize strip between the flanking column and the editor to adjust the column width. The width is saved automatically.

### Collapsing flanking columns

Each flanking column has a small collapse button (▶/◀ arrow) at the top of the resize strip. Click it to collapse the column with a smooth animation. Click again to expand. This lets you quickly clear the screen without changing your panel placement settings.

When a column is collapsed:
- The collapse button changes direction to indicate it can be expanded
- The resize handle is disabled until you expand the column
- The column resumes its previous width when expanded

---

## Right sidebar tabs

The tab bar at the top of the right sidebar shows only the panels that are currently placed in the sidebar. When you move a panel to a flanking column or hide it, its tab disappears automatically.

If the tab you were viewing is moved out of the sidebar, Constellation automatically switches to the first available tab — so you never see a blank sidebar.

---

## Workspaces and panel layouts

Panel placements are saved as part of your workspace. When you save a workspace (via the Workspaces panel), the current panel layout is included. Switching workspaces restores the saved layout.

If a workspace was saved before the panel placement system was introduced, loading it leaves the current layout unchanged.

---

## Tips

- **"Note as organism"**: Placing Backlinks on the left and Outgoing Links on the right puts your note at the center of its link network — you can see what flows in (left) and what flows out (right) at a glance.
- **Focus mode**: All flanking columns and the right sidebar are automatically hidden when Focus mode is active. Your placement settings are preserved and restored when you leave Focus mode.
- **Split view**: Flanking panels are hidden during split view to maximize the available editing space.
