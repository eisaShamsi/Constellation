## 3. The Boss authored the taxonomy — and it corrected mine

**Boss:** *"I think we should have three kinds of templates: 1. A whole note. 2. Just
the frontmatter. 3. A snippet. Did I miss something?"*

The **frontmatter-only** kind was not a subdivision of my snippet-vs-scaffold binary —
it unlocked an *action* that binary had missed entirely: apply properties to the note
you are already in. I had been thinking in shapes; he was thinking in acts.

Then he added the fourth himself: *"You've read my mind. I want to include the fourth
kind… a user wants to start a book project; the studio will walk them through choosing
the title and the book structure, and deciding whether to use existing notes as
chapters or create them from scratch. The Studio shall build the TOC… Of course, the
book is just an example of many other types."*

**Boss:** *"Let's study and research the wizard, and we will decide upon the results."*

Result: of 8 leading long-form tools, **zero** use a step-by-step wizard. The
distinction that reconciles the finding with his standing anti-wizard ruling —
**rail** (decides the steps before meeting you) vs **proposal** (offers a complete
editable structure derived from you). The manuscript builder is a **proposer**.

## 4. The correction that mattered most

Asked whether New Note should offer a template chooser, I researched it and concluded
*"Constellation is in the note camp, 15–0."*

**Boss:** *"Wrong assumption! Constellation is a PKM/PKF system. It is based on the
Cognitive Knowledge making/formulation, and it could use long documents or short notes,
to formulate one's knowledge."*

The error was **categorical** — I imported an outside taxonomy and reasoned from its
habits instead of reading our own concept. The corrected law: design per **cognitive
act** (capture · compose · formulate), not per app category. "New Note stays blank"
survived with a *stronger* reason — that gesture IS the capture gesture, and in
formulation you frequently do not yet know what the thing is. This promoted
*apply-a-template-later* to **the bridge between capture and composition**.

He also flagged a ruling I had walked past: *"I was referring to the ruling that I
bypassed."*

Decisions: **D1** blank · **D2** yes · **D3** yes · **D4** yes.
