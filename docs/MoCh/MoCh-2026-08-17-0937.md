## Boss test — PJ-278…283 (the ①+② remediation)

> **Boss:** "Steps 1-3: Pass. **Step 4: Cannot comply. There is NO tabs in Focus mode. You should
> know that.**"

The step asked him to switch tabs while in Focus mode. It had been auditor-written, inspector-gated
and APPROVED, citing a real effect that fires on a tab change while in Focus. The effect exists and
is defensive — but **no user can reach it**, because the Focus surface is fixed to the whole window
and covers everything. The gate had verified *existence* and never asked *reachability*.

My first instinct was to look for another route to force that state. The Boss shut that down too:

> **Boss:** "You won't find anything to switch to another note or tab in Focus mode. **We design it
> this way, and that's why we name it 'Focus mode'.**"

That is the more important of the two sentences. A surface's constraint **is** its design — the
right response to "the test cannot be performed" is not to engineer around the constraint so the
test can run. Recorded as **LL-045**.

> **Boss:** "Step 5: Pass."

Then, on the Cataloger screenshot: **"pass."**
